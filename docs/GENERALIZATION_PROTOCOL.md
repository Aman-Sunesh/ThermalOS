# ThermalOS v3 Prospective Multi-State System Transfer

## Design

Development: Miami-Dade, FL and Houston/Harris County, TX.

Prospective blind: Phoenix/Maricopa County, AZ; Atlanta/Fulton County, GA; Los Angeles/Los Angeles County, CA.

Houston cannot be called blind because its outcomes were inspected during development.

## Why the protocol changed

A pre-blind development diagnostic tested whether source-consistent FortyGuard satellite morphology could reconstruct local temperature-anomaly/hotspot ordering across Miami and Houston. Magnitude error was modest, but ranking transport was weak across model families, target definitions, and spatial-support choices. The production system therefore does not use morphology-only temperature prediction as a prospective success criterion.

FortyGuard is the temperature-intelligence layer. ThermalOS is the action/decision layer.

## Frozen object

The object frozen before blind-city opening is the complete decision architecture:

1. city data contract and feature assembly;
2. HeatLens thermal burden;
3. portable morphology schema and intervention suitability;
4. literature-bounded intervention priors;
5. candidate generation;
6. budget/equity-constrained MILP;
7. robustness resampling/re-optimization;
8. Policy Stress Lab;
9. evidence/provenance ledger;
10. ThermalVerify protocol and downstream reporting behavior.

The freeze manifest stores code/config hashes and development replay results. Blind evaluation refuses to run if the frozen pipeline or registered city configs change.

## Morphology contract

Exactly 15 active satellite samples per AOI, chosen by the predeclared temperature+spatial maximin strategy. Historical successful calls may be archived, but only the fixed active set enters the registered data contract.

Portable schema:

- fg_canopy_fraction
- fg_vegetation_fraction
- fg_pervious_fraction
- fg_impervious_fraction
- fg_building_fraction
- fg_road_fraction

## Predeclared blind gates

- complete real/non-synthetic data contract;
- unique tile identity;
- complete thermal, exposure, vulnerability, and portable morphology fields;
- exact registered morphology sample count;
- candidate generation succeeds;
- MILP feasible and within budget;
- equity floor satisfied;
- robustness worlds 100% feasible;
- Policy Stress Lab scenarios 100% feasible;
- preregistered equity frontier 100% feasible;
- evidence ledger has at least 10 layers and >=99% minimum layer coverage.

Portfolio stability and median Jaccard are reported, not threshold-tuned after blind opening.

## Blind-open integrity

Blind cities are evaluated only after `scripts/freeze_system_transfer.py` writes a production manifest. `scripts/evaluate_blind_system_transfer.py` verifies hashes, refuses development/synthetic data, and writes a persistent open log. Repeated runs require an explicit debugging override and must not be reported as prospective blind results.

## Claim boundary

This protocol evaluates system portability, not causal intervention-effect transportability. Intervention effects remain literature-bounded planning scenarios. FortyGuard temperature outputs are observed/model-derived upstream inputs to ThermalOS, not labels generated by ThermalOS itself.
